Plans page

The whole lineup on one page: province selector, this month's deal, six monthly plans, the annual plan, and the two buying paths.

Open the live page ↗

How it behaves

  • Province selector (the white pill in the dark band): sets the province for the whole visit. Quebec is deliberately not offered; the list follows Ztar's supported provinces.
  • I have a phone / I need a SIM: the two buying paths. "I have a phone" plus Add now sends the customer to the plan's own page, where the phone check lives, with their province carried along. "I need a SIM" plus Add now adds the plan straight to the cart, pairs the right 911 fee, flashes "Added" on the button and updates the cart count.
  • Best Deal: the dark flagged card. The flag lives on the plan product itself, so it moves when you flag a different plan (see Prices and deals).
  • Annual plans: their own grid below the monthly one, fed by the Annual plans collection. The annual card charges the full year's 911 fee, correctly.
  • Links that open here: any "See annual plans" button on the site opens this page scrolled straight to the annual section.

Edit it, step by step

Open the editor on the plans page.
Headings and intro: click the heading section at the top of the grid ("Choose your plan"). The panel has Eyebrow label, Heading and Subheading fields.
The deals band: click the grey band ("The $39 plan is our best deal right now."). Its panel has Eyebrow label, Heading, Body text, and the Button label and Button URL for the See all deals pill. When the monthly deal changes, this is one of the two places to update (the other is the homepage bar; see Prices and deals).
The grids: click a plan grid section. The Plans collection setting points it at Monthly plans (or Annual plans for the lower grid), and the Helper prefix text / Helper link text / Helper suffix text fields form the small line under the grid. The plan cards themselves are not edited here: their price, allowance and ticks come from the plan products.
Card order: to reorder the plan cards, go to admin → Products → Collections → Monthly plans, and drag the products into the order you want. The grid follows the collection's order.
Press Save and check the live page.
Don't move the province logic The dark province band and the two path buttons are wired to the buying flow. Reordering or hiding that section will confuse customers mid-purchase. Edit its wording if needed; leave its position alone.
