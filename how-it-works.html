How the store works

The five rules that run through every page. Read this first: once you know them, everything else in this guide makes sense.

Rule 1: Province first

The customer picks their province once, on the plans page or on a plan's own page. That single choice follows them through the whole visit: it sets their provincial 911 fee, it rides into the cart, and it carries into activation. The provinces on offer follow the list Ztar's systems support.

Rule 2: One plan per order

A cart holds exactly one plan. If the customer adds a second plan, it replaces the first, along with its fee. Plan quantities are locked to one. The store cleans all of this up by itself, so nobody can buy two plans in one order by accident.

Rule 3: The 911 fee is automatic

Every plan is paired with the government-mandated E911 fee for the customer's province, at the exact amounts Ztar provided. The fee adds itself, corrects itself when the province changes, leaves with the plan, and cannot be bought or removed on its own. Annual plans collect the whole year's fee at once.

Rule 4: The phone check is live

When a customer brings their own phone, the store sends the phone's IMEI (its 15-digit serial number) to Ztar's device-check service the moment it's typed. A compatible phone gets a green confirmation. A phone Ztar can't confirm gets an honest amber note and may continue. An incompatible phone is stopped with a clear message. The store never guesses: it shows exactly what Ztar's service answers.

Rule 5: Buying and activating are two steps

Checkout pays for the plan. Activation, on its own page, is what turns the SIM into a working phone number: it talks to Ztar's activation service and shows the customer their new number. The customer signs in with an email code first, so the activation belongs to their account. Autopay and plan management live in the account portal, after activation.
