Homepage

The front door: hero, plan grid, annual promotion, this month's deal, trust points and reviews. Every part of it is editable without code.

Open the live page ↗

What's on it

  • Hero ("Wireless made simple"): image or video background, heading, and two buttons.
  • Plan grid: the monthly plans, pulled live from the Monthly plans collection. Change a plan's price in the catalog and this updates by itself.
  • Annual band: the annual plan promotion, fed by the Annual plans collection.
  • Deal of the month bar (top of page) and the offer band: pure text and link settings.
  • Why SpeakOut reasons: icon cards with heading and body per card.
  • Buy in store band: the dark band pointing customers to a 7-Eleven, with step text and buttons.
  • Reviews: a carousel of customer quotes. Currently placeholder text, waiting for real quotes.
  • Footer: menus, social icons, legal links. Edited via menus, not the page (see Navigation and menus).

Edit it, step by step

Open the editor on the homepage (it opens on Home page by default).
Change the hero: click the big top section. On the right you'll see the slide's image picker, the heading and text fields, and the two button label and link fields. To use a video instead of an image, the same panel offers a video setting. Type, pick, Save.
Change the deal bar text: click the black bar at the very top. Edit its message and the "See deals" link in the panel.
Change which plans show: click the plan grid section. The panel's Plans collection setting decides which collection feeds it. You'll rarely change this; to change the plans themselves, edit the products (see Create a new plan).
Edit a "why" reason card: in the left panel find the SpeakOut Why Reasons section, open it, and click one of its blocks. Each card has Heading, Body text and an Icon image setting.
Replace the reviews: click the Testimonials section, then a review block. Each has Text, Author name and Author position fields, plus a star toggle. Replace the placeholder quotes with real ones as they come in.
Press Save. Open the homepage in a normal tab and confirm it looks right.
Good to know The reviews carousel supports trackpad swiping as well as its arrows. And the hero looks best with images at least 1800 pixels wide; smaller images will look stretched.
