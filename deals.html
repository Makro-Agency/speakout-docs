Deals

The offers page: hero, live deal cards, and notes. Built so marketing can launch and retire offers without a developer.

Open the live page ↗

How it behaves

  • Hero: eyebrow, heading, subline and background images (a desktop one and a mobile one).
  • Offer cards: each live deal is its own card with text and a link, usually pointing at the plans page or a specific plan.
  • Reached from: the header Deals link, the homepage deal bar, and the deals band on the plans page. When an offer changes, update those pointers too; the checklist lives in Prices and deals.

Edit it, step by step

Open the editor on the deals page.
Hero: click it; the panel has Eyebrow, Heading, Subline and the two background image pickers. There's also a Show section toggle if you ever want the hero off.
A deal card: click it, edit its heading, note text and link. To retire a deal, hide or remove its block; to launch one, use Add block inside the offers section, fill it in, done.
Press Save and check the live page.
