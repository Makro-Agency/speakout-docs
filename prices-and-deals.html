Prices and deals

The three routine marketing moves: change a price, move the Best Deal flag, and roll the deal of the month.

Change a plan's price

Open admin → Products, open the plan, change the price in the Pricing box, Save.
That's the storefront done: card, plan page and cart all follow. But pricing is shared with Ztar: their variant bills the customer, so a price change must happen on both sides together. Agree it with Ztar/Makro before flipping it, not after.

Move the Best Deal flag

Open the plan currently flagged, scroll to Metafields, switch Best deal off. Save.
Open the newly best plan, switch its Best deal on. Save. The dark flagged card moves with it, on the plans page and the homepage grid. Only ever one at a time; if two are flagged, the store shows the first and ignores the second.

Roll the deal of the month

The deal message appears in three places. Update all three so the story matches everywhere:

The announcement bar (every page, black bar at top): theme editor → click the bar → edit message and link. See Navigation and menus.
The plans page deals band: theme editor on the plans page → click the grey band → Heading, Body text, button label and URL.
The deals page: retire the old offer's card, add the new one. See Deals.
Checklist habit Bar, band, page: three edits, two minutes, and the whole store tells one story. Skipping one is how a "$39 best deal" bar ends up above a "$29" deals page.
