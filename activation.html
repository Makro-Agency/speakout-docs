Activating a SIM

The step that turns a SIM into a working phone number: a two-page flow connected live to Ztar's activation service.

Open the live page ↗

The flow, exactly as the customer walks it

Entry: on Activate your SIM, the customer types the activation code from their SIM package (9 characters) or the SIM card number itself (19 to 20 digits), picks their province, and continues. A wrong-format entry is caught immediately with a red line under the field. The Redeem a voucher card hands off to the account portal (its button goes live when the portal address is confirmed).
Sign in: the form is behind account sign-in: the customer enters their email and the 6-digit code it receives. No password exists to forget; new customers are creating their account in this same step.
The form: city (the list loads live from Ztar for the chosen province), address, phone IMEI (checked live, same messages as the plan page), and a 4-digit account PIN of their choosing.
Activate: the store submits to Ztar's activation service. On success the form gives way to "You're connected." with the customer's new phone number, straight from Ztar's response, and a button to their account. On failure everything they typed is kept and a clear banner asks them to try again.

Edit it, step by step

Open the editor on the entry page: headings, the two cards' copy, and the "what happens next" row are all section settings.
The form page's headings and the success wording (Success heading, Success body, Success button) are settings on the activation form section: open the editor on /pages/activation to reach them.
Field labels and error wording are translation strings; ask Makro for changes there.
Good to know City lists exist only for provinces Ztar has provisioned; a province without them cannot complete activation ("Unable to load cities"), which is being aligned with Ztar. And the success button will point at the Ztar account portal, where autopay and plan management live, once the portal's address is confirmed.
