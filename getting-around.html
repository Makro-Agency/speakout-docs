Getting around the editor

The one skill behind ninety percent of edits: opening the theme editor, finding the thing you want to change, and saving. Five minutes to learn, used everywhere in this guide.

Open the theme editor

Sign in to Shopify admin. If you're unsure of the address, it is admin.shopify.com/store/7-eleven-17155. Use your own staff login.
In the left menu, click Online Store. The Themes screen opens, showing the live theme at the top.
Press the green Customize button on the live theme. The theme editor opens: your store in the middle, a list of the page's sections on the left, and settings on the right once you select something. Shortcut for later: this link opens the editor directly, and every page guide in this documentation includes a link that opens the editor already on the right page.

Find the page you want

At the top center of the editor there is a dropdown that says Home page. Click it.
Pick the page type: Pages for Coverage, Switch, Deals, Help and the others; Collections for the plans and phones listings; Products for a plan's or phone's own page. Then pick the specific one.
The middle of the screen now shows that page exactly as customers see it.

Understand sections and blocks

Every page is a stack of sections (the hero, a text band, the FAQ list). Some sections contain blocks: repeatable items inside them, like one FAQ question, one icon card, one slide. The left panel lists them in order.

  • To edit: click the section or block, either in the left list or directly on the page. Its settings open on the right: text fields, image pickers, link fields, toggles.
  • To reorder: drag the six-dot handle next to its name in the left panel.
  • To hide: click the eye icon next to its name. Hidden is reversible; it's the safe way to try things.
  • To add a block: inside a section in the left panel, click the small Add block line (for example, a new FAQ question).

Save, and what saving means

Press Save, top right. Your change is live for customers immediately. There is no separate publish step for content edits.
Made a mess? The editor's undo arrow (top left) works until you leave. And anything you only hid can be unhidden the same way.
You cannot break the machinery Text, images, links, reordering and hiding are always safe. The buying logic (fees, one plan per order, the phone check) does not live in these settings, so no editor change can break it. The only genuinely sensitive area is the catalog plumbing, covered in Products, plans and fees.
