Why SpeakOut

The pitch page: the value story, reasons to choose SpeakOut, and paths into buying or activating.

Open the live page ↗

What's on it

  • Hero with heading and two buttons.
  • Reason cards (icon, heading, body per card).
  • Dark bands with a message and call-to-action button, including the buy-in-store band with its numbered step text.
  • A plans call-to-action leading into the plan grid.

Edit it, step by step

Open the editor on this page.
Click the hero to change its image, heading and the two buttons' labels and links.
Click a reason card (a block inside the reasons section) to edit its Heading, Body text and Icon image. Add block adds another card; the eye icon hides one.
Click a dark band to edit its Eyebrow label, Heading, Body text and buttons. The buy-in-store band also has a Step text setting for its numbered line.
Save, then open the live page and read it top to bottom once: story pages are the ones where a stray edit reads oddest, and a one-minute read catches it.
