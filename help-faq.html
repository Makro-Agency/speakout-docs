How it's organized

Each category ("Important Notice", "Activation", "SIM cards"...) is its own section on the page. Each question is a block inside its category: click the question, the answer folds out. The page is long by design; customers arrive by scrolling or from search.

Edit a question or answer, step by step

Open the editor on the Help page.
In the left panel, find the category section by its name and click the small arrow to open its block list. Every question in that category is listed.
Click the question you want. The right panel shows two fields: the question line, and the answer (a rich text field: bold, links and lists work).
Edit, Save. The change is live immediately.

Add, remove, reorder

Add a question: open the right category in the left panel, click Add block at the bottom of its list, fill in question and answer, drag it into position, Save.
Remove a question: click the block, then Remove block at the bottom of its settings panel. (Hiding with the eye icon works too, and is reversible: use that when you're not sure.)
Reorder: drag blocks within their category. Whole categories reorder the same way in the page's section list.
Open by default: each question has a toggle to show its answer already expanded: useful for the one announcement everyone should see.
