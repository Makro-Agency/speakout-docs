Working the catalog

Create a new plan

The full recipe, from coordinating with Ztar to seeing the new card live on the plans page.

Do this first Every plan the customer buys maps to a variant on Ztar's own plan product, so their systems can bill and fulfil it. If Ztar hasn't created the matching variant yet, your new plan has nothing to connect to. One message to Ztar (or Makro) before you start saves the round trip.

Step by step

Open admin → Products and click an existing plan close to the new one: say 5 GB plan.
Press Duplicate (top right). In the dialog, name the duplicate its real name (for example "10 GB plan"), keep images ticked, and create it as a draft. The duplicate carries everything that matters: the plan template, the "Plan page" product type, and all the plan settings.
On the new product, set the price (in the Pricing box) to the monthly price.
Scroll to Metafields at the bottom and update, one by one:
  • Data allowance: the line under the price on every card and page, for example "10 GB at 4G speed".
  • Included features: the tick list ("Unlimited Canada-wide Calling" and so on). Usually unchanged.
  • Billing period: monthly, or annual for a yearly plan.
  • Best deal: off. Only one plan carries the flag at a time (see Prices and deals to move it).
  • Ztar variant: the connection from the warning above. Click it and pick the matching variant on the "Unlimited Talk & Text Plan" product. If you don't see the right variant, stop here and ask: this is the one field that must be right.
In the right column under Collections, add the product to Monthly plans (or Annual plans).
To place its card in the grid: open the collection (admin → Products → Collections → Monthly plans), set sorting to Manual if it isn't, and drag the new plan into position. The plans page follows this order exactly.
Back on the product, flip the status from Draft to Active. Save.
Verify like a customer: open the plans page: the card is there with the right price and allowance. Click into it: the four steps work. Add it to the cart with a test province: the right 911 fee pairs itself. That's a healthy plan.
