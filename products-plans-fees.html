Working the catalog

Products, plans and fees

The catalog has three kinds of citizens. Two are yours to edit freely. One set is load-bearing: it makes the Ztar integration work.

Edit freely

  • Plan pages (500 MB plan, 1 GB plan...): products of type "Plan page". Price, title, allowance text, feature ticks, billing period and the Best Deal flag live on the product and its metafields. Change them and every page updates.
  • Phones and accessories: normal Shopify products. Add, price and photograph them the standard way.

The load-bearing set: change only by agreement

ItemWhat it doesSafe to edit?
"Unlimited Talk & Text Plan" productZtar's own product. Its variants are what orders actually carry, so Ztar's systems can read every order.Ztar only
"E911 Fees" productOne variant per province at the government amounts, plus annual variants. Feeds the automatic fee pairing.By agreement
Province entries (metaobjects)Which provinces are offered, their names, and which fee variant each uses.By agreement
Plan metafields starting with speakout.Connect each plan page to Ztar's variant, its allowance text, billing period and flags.By agreement
Order attribute and property names (intent, billingProvince, imei, _speakout_...)The agreed contract Ztar reads off every order.Never rename
One rule to remember Changing a plan's price or copy is always safe. Deleting products, editing the fee product, or renaming anything technical is not: ask Makro or Ztar first. It takes minutes to check and saves a broken order flow.
